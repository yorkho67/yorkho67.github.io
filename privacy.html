隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

本應用程式僅供開發者本人使用，以唯讀方式存取本人的 Google Drive 檔案並下載至本人電腦。

本應用程式不收集、不儲存、不分享任何使用者資料給第三方。

This app is for the developer's personal use only. It reads the developer's own Google Drive files and downloads them to the developer's own computer. No data is collected, stored, or shared with any third party.
